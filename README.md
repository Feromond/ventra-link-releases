# Ventra Link

Private voice, video and text chat for desktop, iPhone, iPad and Android. Your
identity lives on your devices: no phone number or registration needed.

**Download, see what's new and read how it works at
[ventra.dev/ventra-link](https://ventra.dev/ventra-link).**

## Desktop

Direct links to the newest version:

| Download | For |
| --- | --- |
| [macOS](https://ventra.dev/ventra-link/download/mac) (`.dmg`) | macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon |
| [Windows](https://ventra.dev/ventra-link/download/windows) (`Setup.exe`) | Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) |
| [Linux AppImage](https://ventra.dev/ventra-link/download/linux) | Other Linux distributions (preview) |
| [Debian / Ubuntu](https://ventra.dev/ventra-link/download/deb) (`.deb`) | Debian, Ubuntu and similar (preview, no automatic updates) |

## iPhone and iPad

[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6824746233). Free for
iPhone and iPad on iOS 26 or later.

## Android

Android is in closed testing.
[Request Android access](https://ventra.dev/?topic=android#contact) and you'll
be added to the test. Needs Android 14 or later.

Phones and tablets update through their store. Link one to your account with
your recovery phrase or a passkey, and your history, contacts and calls follow
you there.

## Releases

Every desktop version, with its notes and SHA-256 checksums, is on the
[Releases](https://github.com/Feromond/ventra-link-releases/releases) page.
The notes also list what's coming to iPhone, iPad and Android.

Installed desktop apps check here for new versions and only install updates
that carry a valid signature.
